# $POKE Tokenomics

**Ticker**: $POKE  
**Chain**: Solana  
**Total Supply**: 1,000,000,000

## Allocation (Fair Launch Style)
- 75% Liquidity + Community (Pump.fun or Raydium fair launch)
- 10% Team (12-month linear vest)
- 8% Marketing & Partnerships
- 5% Ecosystem / Future rewards & exclusive packs
- 2% Initial liquidity seeding

## Utility
1. **Vote**  
   Stake $POKE to push any Solana token into the Top 20 prize pool.

2. **Odds Boost**  
   Hold + stake $POKE to increase chances of Rare+ and above:
   - Spark (250k): +8%
   - Charge (1M): +15%
   - Surge (2.5M): +22%
   - Apex (5M): +30%

3. **Fee Share / Burns**  
   A portion of pack revenue is used to buy back and burn $POKE.

## Launch Plan
1. Deploy token on Pump.fun or create Raydium pool
2. Seed initial liquidity
3. Announce + airdrop small amount to early testers
4. Enable staking + voting immediately after launch
